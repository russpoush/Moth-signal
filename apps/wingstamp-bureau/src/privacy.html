Private progress.

Wingstamp Bureau is an offline puzzle game. The native app has no analytics, ads, accounts, purchases, or network services. It saves your Guide me preference, completed routes and best hop counts in local device storage. Removing the app or clearing its data removes that progress.

No personal information is requested. No device permissions are needed for gameplay. The fictional moths and postal rules are not biological claims.

This is a development test build, version 0.2.0. It has not been submitted to the App Store or Google Play. Hosted browser previews are subject to the hosting service’s own access and request logging.
