How the Bureau works

  1. Tap a connected perch. Your paper moth follows a dotted line and adds that perch’s large letter to the receipt.
  2. Watch the stamp change. The small “then” letter shows what the same perch will stamp on your next visit.
  3. Spell the delivery word. Letters must arrive in order. When the receipt is full, return to the Bureau nest.

No timer. No penalty for undo. A hint can help with a tricky return visit.
